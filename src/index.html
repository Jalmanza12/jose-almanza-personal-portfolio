01 / Operational analytics

RouteRight Power BI Practice Project

A structured first pass at turning a route-operations workbook into a decision-ready Power BI experience.

Problem

The report frames how to compare a pre-pilot and pilot snapshot, understand RouteRight adoption, and surface operational, route, client, and risk questions.

Approach

Documented workbook facts, recommended a model and relationships, defined KPIs, and mapped a five-page dashboard plan. The report also flags incomplete August data, missing satisfaction fields, and denominator issues.

Outcome

A Power BI assessment and build plan that makes the next analytical steps explicit while keeping descriptive results separate from claims of causality.

02 / Data analytics

North American Soccer Analytics

An analysis of player profiles across Major League Soccer and Liga MX using FIFA 22 data.

Question

How do player roles, ratings, wages, values, ages, and league context relate across North American soccer?

Approach

Cleaned and prepared the dataset, reviewed missing values and outliers, transformed wage and value fields, grouped player roles and age bands, and explored correlations, distributions, and clusters.

Outcome

A report with descriptive statistics, visual analysis, and k-means clustering intended to reveal patterns in player profiles and compensation.

03 / Predictive modeling

Loan Approval Classification

A finance-focused classification paper built around the question of what drives home-loan approval outcomes.

Problem

Identify influential applicant and financial factors and evaluate how accurately a model can predict loan approval outcomes.

Approach

Prepared the loan-sanction dataset, handled missing values, encoded categorical fields, partitioned the data, and evaluated a logistic regression model.

Outcome

The report highlights credit history as the most influential factor in the model and documents the modeling process, fit metrics, and recommendations.

04 / Business analytics

Key Factors Influencing Ford Vehicle Price

A prediction project focused on pricing signals in a used Ford vehicle inventory.

Question

Which vehicle attributes are most associated with price, and how can those signals inform pricing and marketing decisions?

Approach

Explored missing values, outliers, distributions, correlations, and non-linear relationships before comparing regression models with and without interactions.

Outcome

The report identifies year, mileage, and engine size as important price-related variables and documents model evaluation and recommendations for inventory strategy.
